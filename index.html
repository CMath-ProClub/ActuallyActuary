How I think about this

  • Risk tolerance is the part everybody skips, and it quietly decides everything else. Work out what you can actually sit through before you work out what you want to own.
  • I am eighteen. Time is the one real advantage I have, so I would rather hold through a bad stretch than try to time my way around it. Time in the market beats timing the market, and I have gone looking for the exception.
  • Diversification is not there to make the good years bigger. It is there so that capital survives to compound, which is the whole point.
  • A backtest that only ever agrees with you is not a test.
  • The interesting number is never the return. It is the worst stretch you would have had to sit through without quitting.
